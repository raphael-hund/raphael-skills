## Patterns to use, with conditions

### P01 — Make the offer concrete early

Observed across the [solar study](service-sites-solar.md), [Leadfluss/Faris](service-sites-agencies.md) and [specialists](service-sites-specialists.md): the opening links a customer outcome to an identifiable service and action. Later blocks explain how the business delivers it.

Adaptation: give the hero a specific audience/service/territory where relevant, a useful outcome, a real visual and an understandable next step. Place a relevant proof item nearby if available. If proof is missing, use verified scope, process and people; do not invent ratings. A bold headline still needs an immediately understandable offer. Aggressive pain language is optional and often unsuitable for sensitive local services.

### P02 — Match proof to the decision being made

Solar references expose project parameters; JANtronic links testing/documentation; property references show properties and people; agencies show work and contextualised client stories. See [solar](service-sites-solar.md), [specialists](service-sites-specialists.md), [SEO Labs clients](service-sites-seolabs.md).

Adaptation: put the proof next to the claim it supports. A project record can contain the real task, location, constraints, solution and attributable result. A technical service may need a document sample; a personal service may need the responsible expert. Distinguish documentary photos from decorative imagery. Repeat the same factual dataset consistently across pages rather than hardcoding competing counters.

### P03 — Section order follows unresolved questions

The references combine offer, problem, service explanation, evidence, people, process and enquiry in different orders. Rollers also needs selection and specifications; Haubner needs separate seller and buyer journeys. They do not all share one optimal layout. [Agency/local study](service-sites-agencies.md).

Adaptation: brief a section as **question → answer → appropriate content form → next step**. Examples: “Does this suit my roof?” calls for suitability criteria and a project; “Who handles installation?” calls for people and responsibilities. Choose a comparison, image/text pair, project list or timeline to fit that answer. Do not replace all of them with interchangeable feature cards or enforce a fixed section count.

### P04 — Use different page families for different jobs

| Page family | Core job | Useful composition to adapt |
|---|---|---|
| Homepage | Orient and direct | Offer, relevant evidence, service choices, distinguishing delivery, people/process, next step |
| Service hub | Help choose | Clear category boundaries, suitability/selection criteria, links to real detail pages |
| Service detail | Resolve purchase uncertainty | Specific outcome, suitability, scope, process, relevant proof, costs/constraints where known, service FAQ, enquiry |
| Local page | Establish genuine relevance | Actual service availability, local projects/context, practical logistics and route to a responsible person |
| Project detail | Make experience assessable | Context, constraint, chosen work, authentic images, documented result and related service |
| About/team | Explain capability and responsibility | Named roles, customer-relevant experience, approach, evidence and contact |
| Guide/comparison | Support a real decision | Direct answer, tradeoffs, criteria, examples, sources and contextual service links |
| Contact/configurator | Make the next step usable | Expected outcome, necessary inputs, honest process, validation and a real destination |

These are starting compositions, not required templates. Learn from [Rollers](service-sites-agencies.md) and [JANtronic](service-sites-specialists.md) for specification-heavy work and [SEO Labs clients](service-sites-seolabs.md) for local services. A brief for a regional installer should not inherit Enpal's entire knowledge base or Priwatt's shop. The agreed page map still governs complete implementation.

### P05 — Write specific copy before decorative copy

Across the studies, useful copy connects a recognisable situation with a service, a concrete mechanism or deliverable, an uncertainty it resolves and a next action. An outcome headline without the mechanism leaves the offer vague. A technical list without the customer consequence leaves relevance unexplained.

Adaptation: state what the customer gets and what the business actually does. Describe responsibilities, deliverables and the first conversation. Use consistent Du/Sie and market spelling. Earn claims such as “fast”, “complete” or “experienced” through facts. Reuse the argument structure, not another company's wording, guarantee, urgency counter, testimonial or performance number. Avoid copy that praises the website's design when visitors came to buy the client's service.

### P06 — Use interaction to reduce uncertainty

Candidate jobs found in the source studies include product/service choice, comparison, project discovery, a multi-step enquiry, specification retrieval, process explanation and FAQ disclosure. Text establishes a candidate, while [visual/state evidence](service-sites-visual.md) establishes only the behaviour actually exercised.

Adaptation: prioritise a useful navigation, product comparison, suitability selector, real gallery or clear enquiry flow. Keep motion proportional: feedback, disclosure and continuity can make an ordinary service page feel carefully made. Use 21st.dev for those component jobs according to [21st-dev.md](21st-dev.md). Do not import animation merely because a library offers it. Keyboard, mobile and reduced-motion support remain requirements for the new implementation even when the reference has not been tested for them.

### P07 — Connect commercial pages with useful information

Enpal/Priwatt expose decision content; Rollers connects equipment with specifications/training; JANtronic connects news with procurement consequences; SEO Labs separates services, sectors and evidence. [Solar](service-sites-solar.md), [agencies](service-sites-agencies.md), [specialists](service-sites-specialists.md), [SEO Labs](service-sites-seolabs.md).

Adaptation: create and interlink pages for distinct intent and actual business capability. Resolve which service a guide supports and which proof supports a service. Useful local pages need local substance; a city name in a heading is insufficient. Validate raw HTML, canonicals, metadata, status, schema and internal links independently. A source's attractive design, large sitemap or calculator is not evidence of Google ranking causality.

## What the positive references do not license

- Do not promote a pattern to a rule merely because several related client sites repeat it.
- Do not treat duplicate extracted headings, zero-valued counters or hidden panel text as visible defects without browser confirmation.
- Do not copy inconsistent numbers, placeholder fragments, transplanted location text or unverified promotional claims. The source studies retain concrete examples to check.
- Do not infer source libraries, easing, exact timing or mobile behaviour from a screenshot or branded hosting asset.
- Do not make every website visually quiet. Preserve a selected source's relevant colour roles, typographic contrast, imagery and density using the client's own identity. Calm, bold and technical can each suit a service business.

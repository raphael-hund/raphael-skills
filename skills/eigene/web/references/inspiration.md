## Turn browsing into executable direction

1. **Choose a small coherent set.** Start with two or three whole-site references matched to audience, trust needs and content density; add a relevant mobile example and only the detail references needed to settle specific questions. A local professional service and an experimental portfolio may need different visual intensity and information hierarchy.
2. **Open actual examples.** Follow the original live website, not just its thumbnail. Inspect the relevant page, section and interaction at matching desktop/mobile widths. Record loading, motion and open-menu states when they affect the decision. If only a screenshot is available, label it static evidence.
3. **Describe observations concretely.** Capture source URL, screenshot path, date, viewport/state, observed relationship, intended page/section, adaptation and confidence. Where measurable, note type scale, content width, spacing, crop, colour role and timing. Mark estimated values as estimates; do not invent CSS inspection.
4. **Select, adapt and reject.** Explain why the reference fits this brand and visitor task. Adopt the useful relationship—such as strong editorial hierarchy or a clear service index—through the project's own fonts, assets, copy and tokens. Record mismatches and rejected patterns as well as selected ones.
5. **Write DESIGN.md.** Translate the chosen direction into the executable design contract in [design-contract.md](design-contract.md). Keep reference observations, intended decisions and measured implementation distinct. A catalogue of links alone is not a design system.
6. **Compare running results.** Give an independent critic source and implementation screenshots at equivalent viewports and states. Review full pages as well as details. Identify observable gaps in hierarchy, imagery, spacing, readability and mobile behaviour; fix them and capture fresh evidence. See [quality-agents.md](quality-agents.md).

A popular gallery, beautiful static shot or award cannot prove rankings, conversion rates, accessibility or fast loading. Verify those claims separately. Preserve attribution and asset/code licences. Reuse permitted components selectively, restyle them to the brand and test their states. Decorative effects must leave useful content readable, crawlable and operable, including keyboard and reduced-motion use. Read [creator-methods.md](creator-methods.md) for the bounded creator evidence behind this reference-led workflow.
